# Simplified Chinese (zh) glossary

## Address and typography

- The reader is 你 (never 您). The program speaks plainly: 图书馆会……, 你可以……
- Full-width punctuation in Chinese text: ，。：；？！（）、 and “ ” for quotations (‘ ’ inside them).
  A list of words is joined with 、. The ellipsis character … stays where the English has it.
- Spacing: Chinese is glued to numbers and to placeholders that hold a number or a Chinese word
  (“{0}个来源”, “共{0}条”; the seconds fragment " s" is “秒”, so “3.2秒”).
  One half-width space goes on each side of a Latin word, a code span, a /command, a --flag, a setting
  name, a file name, an address, an id, a model or program name, and of a placeholder that holds one of
  those (“在终端运行 {0}。”, “用 OpenAI 兼容的地址”). No space next to full-width punctuation or at the
  ends of a string.
- Numbers: Arabic where the English writes digits, Chinese numerals where the English spells a small
  number out (“十到二十秒”, “六位数验证码”). Money as the English writes it (“$1.50”, “${0}”).

## Counts (rule 4)

Chinese nouns do not change with the number, so the "one" and the "many" form are the same text, with
the measure word the noun wants: {0}个问题 / 个来源 / 个主题 / 个任务 / 个文件 / 个模型 / 个候选,
{0}条论断 / 条留言 / 条术语, {0}份报告 / 份档案 / 份文档, {0}次调查 / 次搜索 / 次调用,
{0}项 for settings and features, {0}页 for pages, {0}位 for people when counted politely
(“{0}个人” where plain), {0}天, {0}分钟, {0}秒, {0}字.

Split keys: Keep (button) 保存 · Read in (the page that reads material in) 导入, Read in (a report's language line) 阅读语言 ·
Reading (a run's stage) 阅读中, Reading (career column) 解读 · Read (career column) 细读数 · Ask (button) 申请 · deny (button)
拒绝 · set (button) 设置. Also changed: note (with an access request) 留言, asked (an open question's fate, ×N) 有人问过,
records (the years a file's records cover) 记录时段.

The yes/no hint is （是/否）. Typed yes: 是、好、可以、行、对、嗯、要、开始吧…; typed no (the whole answer only): 不、不要、不用、否、
不是、不行、算了…; “不要译注” is read as the notes and never as a no. Pictures, as the help says them: 都保存、保存第1、3张、
哪些图片里有…、找更多这个人的照片、找类似的地方、这是什么？ “in genealogy mode” stays in English: the program reads a field's mode
only by its English name.

Helplines: numbers, service names and hours exactly as given, glued to Chinese by the spacing rule (拨打988), a space next to a
Latin or kana name.

## Third round (the pages that were still English)

- Dossier sections: 基本情况, 解读 (Reading), 所做的事, 检索范围, 时间线, 决定, 原话, 影像 (Media: 媒体 reads as "the press"), 各方面 (Facets), 生活规律 (Pattern of life), 关系, 告知 (Told), 分析, 未找到, 矛盾, 检查, 关联, 共有, 更新, 尚未提供.
- The badges of a claim's source: 公开记录 / 自己发布 / 告知 / 提供 / 第三方 / 原作.
- watch (a dossier) = 跟踪; the plan's clauses are joined as `{0}。{1}`; a service with its description is written "Exa（…）".
