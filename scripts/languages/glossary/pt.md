# Glossary: Brazilian Portuguese (pt)

Address: "você" (verbs in the third person: "Escolha um arquivo", "você pode"). Buttons and menu items in the
infinitive ("Salvar", "Excluir", "Buscar", "Aceitar"). Instructions in running text use the imperative with você
("Escolha", "Digite", "Responda"). Quotation marks “ ”. Decimal comma, thousands dot (1.000; 1,5). Seconds " s",
minutes " min". No inclusive forms: neutral wording ("quem pode escrever", "a pessoa que administra a biblioteca").

## Placeholders

Nothing agrees or contracts with `{0}`: no "do {0}", "no {0}", "o {0}". Use a colon form ("Dossiê: {0}"), the
noun before it ("o assunto “{0}”", "a pesquisa {0}"), or put it where no article is needed.

## Counts (rule 4)

Portuguese has two forms, one for exactly 1 and one for everything else (0 included: "0 afirmações"), which is the
program's rule. The second text is always the plural.

## Counts that are not chosen by the program

Where the English writes "run(s)" or a bare number next to a noun the program does not pick, the Portuguese puts
the noun first with a colon so it holds for every number: "pesquisas por noite: {0}", "aceitas: {0}",
"recusadas: {0} ({1})", "independentes: {0}", "links: {0}".

## Second round

The yes/no hint is now the program's, from the words file: "(s/N)". Answers read as yes: sim, s, claro, pode, ok
(and a few longer forms); as no: não, nao, n, não obrigado; "sem notas" is never a no. The note above about "(y/N)"
no longer applies.

Language names inside a sentence come from the words file, lowercase and without article: "escrito em japonês",
"traduzido para inglês". The help keeps the article where a person types it: “traduza este arquivo para o japonês”.
Standing alone (lists, the language switch) they stay capitalized: Japonês, Inglês.

## Third round (the pages that were still English)

- Told (section, badge, button): Depoimentos / depoimento / “Registrar como depoimento”.
- The class of a claim's source: tipo. Its badges: registro público, publicação própria, depoimento, material fornecido, de terceros, cânone.
- Pattern of life = Padrão de atividade ("padrão de vida" means standard of living); watch (a dossier) = Acompanhar / “Em acompanhamento:”; superseded = substituído; research in full = Pesquisa completa.
