# German (de) glossary

## Form of address and style

- The reader is addressed as **Sie** everywhere in the interface.
- Buttons and menu items: infinitive or noun ("Speichern", "Löschen", "Suche", "Markierte annehmen").
- Quotation marks „…“, inner ‚…‘. No gender star or colon forms; people in general are written neutrally
  ("wer die Bibliothek verwaltet", "wer schreiben darf", "mit Leserecht").
- A placeholder that a noun, adjective or article would have to agree with is put after a colon or in
  quotation marks after its noun: „Thema „{0}““, "Bericht: {0}".
- Numbers with units: "10 s", "5 min", "1,50 $". The program's own figures ({0}) are left as the program writes them.

## Counts (rule 4)

German has two forms like English: singular for exactly 1, plural for every other number, 0 included
("0 Aussagen", "2 Aussagen", "21 Aussagen"). The second text is always the plural.

## What a person types in the chat (rule 6)

The chat is spoken to with "du", as people type to a chat program; the interface itself says "Sie".

## Second round

The yes/no hint is now the program's and reads **(j/N)** (words file `hint.yes_no`); the line above about
"(y/N) stays" no longer applies. j, ja, gern, okay are yes; n, nein, nein danke are no; „ohne Anmerkungen“ is
never a no. The help names „j oder ja“.

A language name the program fills in is the bare name („Japanisch“, the first entry of `lang.ja`), so a
sentence carries it after „auf“ or „Zielsprache“: „geschrieben auf {0}“, „Zielsprache {0}“, „übersetze diese
Datei auf {0}“. The typed forms „ins Japanische“ and „auf Japanisch“ are both understood.

## Third round (the pages that were still English)

- The class of a claim's source: Art der Quelle. Its badges: amtlich / eigene Veröffentlichung / mitgeteilt / bereitgestellt / von Dritten / Kanon.
- statement: Äußerung (Aussage already means claim).
- Dossier sections: Reading = Einschätzung, Told = Mitgeteilt, Facets = Facetten, Pattern of life = Lebensmuster, Coverage = Abdeckung.
- superseded = ersetzt; watch = verfolgen; search by meaning = Suche nach Bedeutung; tilt = Neigung; canon = Kanon; Families (the page of family names) = Familiennamen.
