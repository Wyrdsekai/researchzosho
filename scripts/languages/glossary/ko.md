# Korean (ko) glossary

## Form of address and style

- Sentences and prompts: 합니다/하세요 (polite formal). Buttons and tabs: a noun or a short noun phrase (저장, 삭제, 검색, 모두 채택).
- No 당신. "Your X" on a label is 내 X (내 북마크, 내 책장, 내 계정); in a sentence the subject is left out, or 직접 when it must be said.
  The chat's own turn label "You" is 나.
- The Librarian speaks of itself as 제가 (제가 찾아본 어디에도 없습니다).
- Placeholders: no particle that depends on the final sound right after `{0}`. Used instead: a colon (`주제: {0}`), a prefix with a colon
  (`조사 {0}: 시작했습니다.`), quotation marks with a noun (`“{0}” 질문`), a noun after the placeholder (`{0}용 키`, `{0} 명령`,
  `{0} 버전으로`), or a particle that never changes (의, 에, 에서, 에게, 까지, 부터, 도, 만, 처럼, 입니다, 인).
  The both-forms convention 을(를) / 이(가) / 은(는) / (으)로 is used nowhere.
  Exceptions on purpose: `{0}로` after a language name (every Korean language name ends in 어, so 로 is always right) and a particle after a
  link whose text this file also translates (`{0}를 누르고` with {0} = 페이지 다시 불러오기; `{0}하세요` with {0} = 로그인).
- Quotation marks: “ ” for what a person types or a quoted phrase. The model-naming phrases stay in English inside straight quotes.
- Semicolons in sentences become a full stop or a comma; command syntax keeps its semicolons.
- Numbers with units are written together: 5초, 10분, 3일, 2시간, 100만 토큰당, 약 3,000단어. Ranges with ~ (20~40분).
- Command placeholders are translated as the Japanese does: <이름>, <ID>, <검색어>, <일수>, <언어>, <주제>, <제목>.

## Counts (rule 4)

Korean has no plural, so the "one" and "other" strings are the same: the noun first, then the number with a counter
(`주장 {0}건`, `보고서 {0}건`). Both forms hold for 0, 1, 2, 5 and 21.

| counter | for |
|---|---|
| 건 | claims, reports, listings, openings, facts, research runs, recall notices, results |
| 개 | questions, keys, tools, units, sources, links, glossary entries, models, pairs, things in general |
| 곳 | employers |
| 명 | people, workers |
| 편 | videos |
| 장 | cards, pictures |
| 대 | servers |
| 번 / 회 | times a thing was called |
| 자 | characters |
| 분 / 초 / 시간 / 일 | minutes, seconds, hours, days |

## Second round (337 new strings, the chat's words file)

- The yes/no questions no longer end in (y/N): the program adds the hint from the words file, (네/아니요). Both 네 and 예 are read
  as yes. The note above ("The (y/N) prompts keep y; the text says y나 예") is replaced by this; the sentence beside a hinted
  question says “네”, the older help still says “예”.
- The exception `{0}하세요` with {0} = 로그인 is gone: "Sign in to do this." is a whole sentence now (이 작업을 하려면 로그인하세요.).
- Field names inside "{0} 방식" (Use {0} mode for this question?): career 일자리 찾기, podcasts 팟캐스트, organization 조직,
  science 과학, person 인물, genealogy 가족사, youtube 동영상, books 도서, software 소프트웨어, shopping 쇼핑, patents 특허,
  law 법률, home 집 찾기, crafts 공예. "Genealogy mode" in a sentence is 가족사 방식; the typed "in genealogy mode" stays English.
- The second senses: Keep ⟦save button⟧ 저장 · Reading ⟦career column⟧ 분석 · Read ⟦searches column⟧ 읽은 수 · Ask ⟦request
  button⟧ 요청 · deny ⟦button⟧ 거부 · set ⟦password button⟧ 설정 · Read in ⟦the page⟧ 자료 넣기 (as "Reading material in").
- New terms: the reader model (the one that checks a translation) 점검 모델; the translator 번역 모델; effort 추론 강도;
  cap $X a month 월 상한 $X; a model's tier 단계; search place 검색처; restart 재시작; none of them 해당 없음; a passkey 패스키,
  {0} passkeys 패스키 {0}개; aspect "look and subject" 겉모습과 대상, "mood and look" 분위기와 겉모습, "look and style" 겉모습과
  스타일; kinds 장소, 물건, 동물, 조각상, 그림, 동전, 배; today 오늘, yesterday 어제; every day 매일.
- Durations: 1분 미만, 약 {0}분, 약 {0}시간, 약 {0}시간 {1}분. A tier with its time: 점검 단계로 약 2분. Pictures are counted with 장.
- List joins: "{0} and {1}" {0} 및 {1} (as " and " was), " and on " 및, "; and " ", 그리고 ".
- Typed phrases the new help names, all in the words file: “모두 저장해 주세요” (keep them), “1, 3번 저장해 주세요” (keep 1, 3),
  “이 중 어느 이미지에 … 있나요?” (which of these show …), “이 사람의 이미지를 더 찾아 주세요” (more of this one),
  “이런 장소를 찾아 주세요” (ones like this), “이게 뭐예요?” (what is this), “deepseek 모델을 기본으로 해 주세요” (make X the
  default: 모델 after the name, so no particle hangs on the name).
- Words file: the tiers are the glossary's (간단, 점검, 검토, 전체 검토) with their spaced and unspaced forms; the notes are 주석
  (the interface's word), with 역주 kept as a variant. Translation asks are read only in request forms (번역해, 번역 좀, 번역 부탁,
  옮겨 줘), so "이 책을 영어로 번역한 사람" is not taken for an ask.
