# A Greek name in Greek letters is written in Latin letters by ELOT 743, the Greek standard and the passport's, the first way here:
# Παπαδόπουλος is Papadopoulos, Χρήστος is Christos, Ευάγγελος is Evangelos. Records also write the name as it sounds (Hristos, Kiriakos,
# Irini, Yiorgos) and in the old learned way (Philippos, Euangelos), and Greeks writing Greek in Latin letters online write μπ as mp and γγ as
# gg (Mpakogiannis, Aggelos). These rules say how each sound is written, so those spellings compare as one name.

# comparison: ph and f are one (φ); ch, kh and h are one (χ), and a c that is no s sound is k (Constantinos); y, i and ei are one (υ, ι, ει);
# gi and yi before a vowel are the y sound of γ (Giorgos, Yiorgos, Jorgos); gg is ng; gk, mp and nt at the start are g, b and d; eu and au
# are ev and av (Euangelos), and ou is u. η written e, the ancient way (Demetrios), stays apart from i: with it every e would be an i.
