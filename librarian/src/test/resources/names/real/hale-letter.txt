Notes on my great-grandmother, written from the family Bible and her letters.

Mary Ellis was born Mary Hale in York in 1850, the eldest of the Hales of Micklegate. She married Tom Ellis, a printer, in York in 1875, and signed her letters "M. Ellis" from then on. Tom died in 1886. In 1890 she married John Hart, a widower, and she was Mary Hart until her death in Leeds in 1920.

Hale's daughter Ruth, Mary's younger sister, never married and lived with Mary in Leeds from 1905.
