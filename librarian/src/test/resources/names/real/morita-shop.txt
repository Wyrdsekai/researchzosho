Chapter 3. The Morita shop

The Endō family (遠藤家) had farmed near Kōfu for four generations. Endō Shōichi (遠藤正一) was born in 1875 and married Endō Toku in 1899. Endo's son, Morita Kenji, told us in 1998 that his father kept silkworms and sold the cocoons in Kōfu every autumn.

Kenji was born in 1905 as Endō Kenji (遠藤健二), the second son. He went to the village school in 1912. In 1932 he married Haru (森田ハル), the only daughter of Morita Isamu (森田勇), the head of the Morita family, and entered the family as mukoyōshi (婿養子) of Isamu. From then on he was known as Morita Kenji (森田健二, もりた けんじ).

Kenji ran the Morita shop in Kōfu until 1965. Isamu's son Shōji (森田正二) left the Morita family in 1940, when he was adopted as heir into the Takahashi family (髙橋家) of Nagano, and was known afterwards as Takahashi Shōji (髙橋正二).

Kenji and Haru had one son, Morita Tadashi (森田正), born in 1934. Tadashi's son Masaru (森田勝) was born in 1962. In 1990 Masaru became Takahashi Masaru (髙橋勝) and carried on the Takahashi line.
