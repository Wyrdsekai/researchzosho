Endo's son, Morita Kenji, went to the village school in 1920. In 1932 he entered the Morita family (森田家), whose head was Morita Isamu, as mukoyōshi (婿養子) of Isamu, and married Isamu's daughter Haru. 森田健二（もりた けんじ）ran the family shop for thirty years. Isamu's son Morita Shōji (森田正二) left the Morita family in 1940, when he was adopted as heir into the Takahashi family (髙橋家), and became Takahashi Shōji (髙橋正二). In 1990 Kenji's grandson Morita Masaru (森田勝) became Takahashi Masaru (髙橋勝) and carried on the Takahashi line.
