# Portrait pairs for the likeness helper's calibration

Twenty-six photographs of thirteen people, two sittings each, all in the public domain by Wikimedia Commons's own licence field (read on 2026-11-08 through the Commons API, `extmetadata.LicenseShortName`), fetched once as 320-pixel thumbnails. The same-person pairs are the two files of one person; the different-person pairs cross the first file of one person with the second of the next.
