# The ResearchZosho marks

The emblem, the kura, the wordmark and the name **ResearchZosho** (研究蔵書) identify this project.
They are shipped here so the README, a fork or a mirror can show the project's own mark without
depending on researchzosho.org being up.

**They are not covered by the Apache License** that covers the code. You may use them to refer to
ResearchZosho — a link, a "built with", a screenshot, a patron that speaks its protocol — and you may
not use them, or a confusingly similar mark or name, to identify a different project or product. A
fork that changes what ResearchZosho does should take its own name and mark.

The mark is a brush painting: the double-loop knot the family shares with CodeZaiku, with a kura in
it — 蔵, the storehouse that is the 蔵 in 蔵書 — and one branch of Wyrdsekai's pine over the roof. It
reads down to 48 px. Below that the kura alone stands for it, painted in the same hand; it reads as a
house at 16.

| file | use |
|---|---|
| `emblem-{1024,512,256,128,64}.png` | the mark, on a transparent ground, for light backgrounds |
| `emblem-dark-{1024,…,64}.png` | the mark for dark backgrounds: the ink graded harder and lifted (not inverted), with a lamp lit in the kura's window |
| `emblem-mono.svg` | the mark as a one-colour silhouette (`currentColor`), for stamps, print, the terminal |
| `kura-{256,128,64,32}.png`, `kura-dark-*.png` | the small mark, for 48 px and below |
| `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `favicon-32.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` | the kura as icons |
| `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `maskable-512.png` | the mark as an app icon on the family's cream |
| `wordmark.svg` | the name, set in CMU Serif Italic (SIL Open Font License) |
| `kanji.svg` | 研究蔵書, set in Noto Serif CJK Bold (SIL Open Font License) |
| `logo-light.png`, `logo-dark.png` | emblem, name, kanji and tagline together |
| `github-social.png`, `og-image.png`, `og-image-dark.png` | the wide lockup for cards and previews |

The rule under the lockups is CodeZaiku's, teal to purple: the two are siblings, and Wyrdsekai is the
house both belong to.

The source paintings and the generator that produces these files are not part of the public tree.
